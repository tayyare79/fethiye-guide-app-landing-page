Fethiye Guide

Gizlilik Politikasi / Privacy Policy - Last updated: June 10, 2026

Turkce

Fethiye Guide kisisel veri toplamaz, kullanicilari takip etmez ve reklam/analitik SDK'si icermez.

Favoriler, dil tercihi, inceleme istemi sayaci ve indirilen herkese acik icerik onbellegi yalnizca cihazda saklanir. Uygulamayi silerseniz bu yerel veriler de silinir.

Hava durumu icin Open-Meteo, etkinlik ve icerik guncellemeleri icin fethiye-app.com, nobetci eczane bilgisi icin eczaneler.gen.tr ve nobetcieczaneleri.com kaynaklarina anonim ag istekleri yapilabilir. Bu istekler hesap, kimlik, reklam tanitici veya hassas veri icermez.

Uygulama konum izni istemez; harita sabit Fethiye noktalarini gosterir. Dis baglantilar, telefon, WhatsApp, Instagram veya harita acma islemleri kullanicinin secimiyle gerceklesir ve ilgili hizmetlerin kendi gizlilik kurallarina tabidir.

Veri uygulamalari degisirse bu sayfa ve App Store gizlilik bilgileri guncellenir.

English

Fethiye Guide does not collect personal data, track users, or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Favorites, language preference, review-prompt counters, and cached public content are stored only on the device. Deleting the app removes this local data.

The app may make anonymous network requests to Open-Meteo for weather, fethiye-app.com for events and content updates, and eczaneler.gen.tr plus nobetcieczaneleri.com for duty-pharmacy information. These requests do not contain accounts, identity, advertising identifiers, or sensitive data.

The app does not request location permission; the map shows fixed Fethiye points of interest. External links, phone calls, WhatsApp, Instagram, or map actions happen only when selected by the user and are governed by those services' policies.

If data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy information will be updated.
